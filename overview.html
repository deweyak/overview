Embedding & Dashboard Tips

OmniView renders 13 web services in tandem in a unified screen view. To give you the best out-of-the-box experience, it comes pre-loaded with 13 embed-permissive dashboards, readers, trackers, and widgets.

Why do some websites say "Refused to connect"?

Many websites send HTTP headers called X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN or Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'self'. This prevents unauthorized clickjacking.

When a site blocks framing, click the tile's Open in Tab button or use an official embed URL (like Wikipedia's mobile view, YouTube embed URLs, Google Maps embed URLs, or HackerWeb for HN).
